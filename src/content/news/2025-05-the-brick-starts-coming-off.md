### Restoration
## The first soft deconstruction.

The veneer began coming away by hand, course by course, because a machine would have taken the logs with it. Underneath were hand hewn logs, cut on this ground, sound at both floors.

They had not been seen in about a hundred years. Photographs from both days are on [the restoration page](/the-restoration/).
