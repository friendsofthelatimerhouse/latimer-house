### The record
## The transfer is complete.

The restricted fund Mr. Brown left to Sumner County has been transferred in full to the Friends of the Latimer House at the William and Martha Brown Park. It moved in two payments.

The first, $500,000, was released at the end of August, days after the deed was recorded. The remainder waited on the architect. On September 9 the county paid the architect's final invoice, liquidated the encumbrances that had been held against it, and receipted the August interest, which left a final cash balance of $88,975.37 in the sub-fund. The requisition was submitted the following morning, the ACH was processed on September 10, and the money was in the organization's account on Monday, September 14.

Together the two payments come to $588,975.37. The county's sub-fund is now at zero and the organization holds the whole of it.

### The instrument
## Resolution 2608-07, and an agreement underneath it.

The conveyance and the transfer of the fund were directed by the same instrument: Resolution 2608-07, adopted by the Sumner County Commission on August 17, 2026, and approved by the Sumner County Board of Education on August 18. The deed was recorded on August 27 in Record Book 6928, pages 761 through 769, at the Sumner County Register of Deeds.

The money did not simply change hands. It moved under a Restricted Fund Administration Agreement approved alongside the resolution, which sets out how the organization holds it and what it may be spent on.

### How it got here
## Ten years in the wrong account.

In 2016 Mr. Brown left five hundred thousand dollars to Sumner County to establish a public park with this house as its centerpiece. The money was wrongfully confiscated and combined into the school system's education technology trust, a fund it was never left to.

A small group of county commissioners caught it in 2023 and pressed until it was corrected. The Commission demanded its return by resolution in October 2024. The money was separated back out and returned to the county in March 2025, where it was held in a restricted sub-fund by the county trustee, gathering interest, while the house it was meant for stood under brick.

The fund was the last piece of it.

### What travels with it
## The restriction did not come off.

The money was restricted when Mr. Brown wrote the codicil, and it is restricted now. It can be spent on this house and the ground around it and on nothing else. The deed carries a preservation covenant and a reverter. If the house is not looked after, the house goes back to the county, and so does every unspent dollar.

The organization did not receive a gift it may use as it sees fit. It received a trust with terms, and the terms are enforceable.

### What comes next
## There is already a plan.

The scope, the line item budget and the timeline were built in the spring of 2025 and handed in that June, when the logs were first visible and the work could be priced section by section. That plan is where this starts.

The board will publish an accounting of what the fund is spent on as the work proceeds. The figures above are what came in. What goes out will be shown the same way.

Thank you to everyone who wrote, called, and sat in a room and waited to be heard, in some cases for years. This is what that came to.
