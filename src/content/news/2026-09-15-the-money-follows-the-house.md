### The record
## The transfer is complete.

The restricted fund Mr. Brown left to Sumner County has been transferred to the Friends of the Latimer House at the William and Martha Brown Park. It moved in two parts. The first portion was transferred at the end of August, days after the deed was recorded. The county confirmed on September 10 that the balance would follow, and the funds were in the organization's account by Monday, September 14.

The organization now holds the whole of it.

### How it got here
## Ten years in the wrong account.

In 2016 Mr. Brown left five hundred thousand dollars to Sumner County to establish a public park with this house as its centerpiece. The money was wrongfully confiscated and combined into the school system's education technology trust, a fund it was never left to.

A small group of county commissioners caught it in 2023 and pressed until it was corrected. The Commission demanded its return by resolution in October 2024. The money was separated back out and returned to the county in March 2025, where it was held in a restricted sub-fund by the county trustee. The Commission voted to convey the house on August 17, 2026, the School Board approved it the following day, and the deed was recorded on August 27.

The fund was the last piece of it.

### What travels with it
## The restriction did not come off.

The money was restricted when Mr. Brown wrote the codicil, and it is restricted now. It can be spent on this house and the ground around it and on nothing else. The deed carries a preservation covenant and a reverter. If the house is not looked after, the house goes back to the county, and so does every unspent dollar.

The organization did not receive a gift it may use as it sees fit. It received a trust with terms, and the terms are enforceable.

### What comes next
## There is already a plan.

The scope, the line item budget and the timeline were built in the spring of 2025 and handed in that June, when the logs were first visible and the work could be priced section by section. That plan is where this starts.

The board will publish a full accounting of the fund once the final figures are reconciled with the county. Until then the only figure this organization states is Mr. Brown's five hundred thousand dollars, because that one is settled.

Thank you to everyone who wrote, called, and sat in a room and waited to be heard, in some cases for years. This is what that came to.
